03Head to Head

Where Gorgias wins — and where it doesn't.

🛍 Shopping Assistant — composite #4 of 10 · Envive leads · we lead on quality

✓ Wins

  • Automation 94% — top-tier. Across 6 live V3 stores; only Ada/Rep go higher (100%) and both at near-zero answer quality. Sierra loses 15% of shopping conversations to humans, DigitalGenius 72%.
  • It actually sells — quality now beats Envive (68 vs 64). Head-to-head comparisons, promo terms cited correctly, consistent purchase nudges. Most support-first vendors refuse to recommend at all (DG: 12/100).
  • Zero out-of-channel deflections — never punts the shopper to email/phone (Kodif and Siena both do).

✕ Gaps

  • Speed — 8th of 9 (~18.3s). Sierra 9.5s, Envive 12.6s. Delivery is atomic: the shopper stares at a typing indicator while streaming vendors show substance in 1–5s. The credible bar is 10–12s.
  • Perceived speed, not substance. V3 renders product cards + prices and quality beats Envive (68 vs 64) — but atomic delivery means the shopper waits ~18s at a typing indicator while streaming rivals paint substance in 1–5s.
  • The sale is won on merit, lost on speed. Right answer and rich cards — just delivered too slowly.
🎧 Support — composite #3 of 10 · 67, behind Envive & Ada

✓ Wins

  • Automation 83% — top-tier. The vast majority of engaged support conversations handled with zero human touch. Sierra hands over ~37% of support conversations; the field ranges 55–92%.
  • On configured stores, resolution ties the leaders. Beekman scores 77, Glamnetic 76, Madura 61 — at or above Envive's best (71) and behind only Sierra's Casper (84). The ceiling is top-tier.
  • Latency mid-pack, not last — 14.4s vs. field 7–43s, and 4s faster than our own shopping lane.

✕ Gaps

  • Store-to-store consistency, not capability. Beekman scores 77 but Jade/Shoebacca sit ~41–47 — the ceiling is top-tier, the floor is uneven. Onboarding QA (Config Health Score) closes it.
  • Quality must stay matched to automation. 100% automation only stays a strength if answer quality is uniformly high — Rep AI proves containment alone can hide bad answers.

05Market Learnings

Five things the whole industry should know.

Nobody wins automation × quality × speed — the frontier is open.

Envive is closest (wins on composite, but no order-system depth). Gorgias owns automation, Sierra owns speed + support quality, nobody owns all three.

The vendor that closes its gap first owns the category. Ours is speed; theirs is much harder product work.

The #1 failure mode is not wrong answers — it's walls and loops.

Auth walls (Ada), order-number demands (Kodif, DG), clarification loops (Sierra), upsell loops (Rep AI), handover-everything (Meta/Grove). Judges rarely caught hallucinations; they constantly caught refusal-to-engage.

These are engineering guardrail failures, not model failures — cheap to fix, huge quality upside.

Merchant configuration variance dwarfs vendor model quality.

Same vendor, same model: Meta AI scores 66 on quip and 0 on Grove; Siena 84 on Simple Modern and 0 on FIGS. We caught the same in our own set (a non-functional Gorgias store, excluded).

The battle is won in onboarding and config QA, not in the model. This is a product opportunity.

Speed without substance is a vanity metric — but 18s is still too slow.

Meta AI is 4× faster than us and 9× worse on quality. Still, the quality leaders (Envive, Sierra support) all answer in 9-13s.

~10-12s to a full final answer appears to be the credible bar; we're at 14-18s.

"AI chat" claims ≠ measurable free-text concierge.

Yuma automates tickets behind the scenes (its chat is chip-gated menus); Humind won't load for non-human sessions; 30-40% of widget-detected stores yielded no engageable conversation.

Market noise is high — buyers can't tell. An honest, repeated public benchmark is a sales weapon.
