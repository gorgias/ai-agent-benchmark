Fairness rules baked in

  • A good handover is not a failure. Containment is measured separately by automation rate, so quality never double-penalizes it.
  • Judge only what a first-time visitor could see. Cold session, no account. Never penalized for not knowing your order history.
  • Broken widgets don't count as zero. A conversation needs at least three cleanly-timed answers to count. Dropped connections are store transport, so they're excluded, not scored.
  • Every run is audited. An adversarial second pass re-checks sampled verdicts. A run is trusted only at 90% agreement or higher.

This rubric is the quality score. A vendor's lane ranking also weighs automation rate and speed. Shopping: 40% automation, 35% quality, 25% speed. Support: 50%, 40%, 10%.
